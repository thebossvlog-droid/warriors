1

Welcome — greet every new person

A new woman walks into the live, shy, first night, does not know anyone. You are the first face she sees. Welcome her by name. Tell her she belongs here. Make her feel safe and seen, straight away. Nobody waits to be noticed. Nobody sits alone in my room.

2

Keep it going — interactive, 100% of the time

A Warrior is never just watching. Ask a question. React to what someone said. Keep the chat moving. When the room goes quiet, you talk. When someone shares something, you answer it. The room stays warm because you keep it warm.

3

Grow the team — meet new people, bring them in

Meet new people every night. Encourage others to play. When someone is watching but not playing, invite them in — tap, cheer, answer a Queen Question, tell us about yourself. Point them to the Tell Me About You form, so I get to know them too. Every new Cougar makes the team stronger.
